Kitchen Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and an undersized or failing heater reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater working alongside the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, with service scheduled around your hours.

A dish room without enough hot water can't sanitize, and inspectors notice lukewarm handwashing sinks right away. We keep temperatures and recovery where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to inspectors and landlords.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we install overnight or before opening, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify proper combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Rapid Response

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Yakima Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Yakima and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including gas valve failures, burned-out elements, ignition and flame sensor faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks and fittings, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless error-code lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, isolating a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Yakima Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties that stay on a schedule typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Give us a call to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
